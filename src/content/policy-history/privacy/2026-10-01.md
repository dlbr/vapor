## Data sources and recipients

DLBR receives information directly from you, from the business customer that manages your account or requested a verification, and from the browser or service systems you use. Verification claims come from the wallet or other verification method selected by the customer and are processed under that customer's instructions.

DLBR shares data with the customer whose service you use, infrastructure and communications providers that host or operate DLBR EID, and professional advisers who support DLBR's business. DLBR may disclose data to public authorities when the law requires it. DLBR uses Cloudflare infrastructure for hosting and related platform services, including optional website analytics and transactional email delivery.

DLBR is established in Serbia. Where GDPR applies to customer verification data transferred to DLBR or its providers outside the European Economic Area, the customer and DLBR must document a valid transfer mechanism and any required safeguards in the DPA. Contact [privacy@dlbr.app](mailto:privacy@dlbr.app) for information about the mechanism applicable to your service and how to obtain a copy of relevant safeguards.

## Cookies, browser storage, and analytics

The marketing website stores your cookie choice in browser storage so it can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics. The choice remains until you change it or clear browser storage. The site also stores a Light or Dark display preference if you select one; System is the default and clears that saved preference.

Optional analytics remain off until you select **Accept optional** or enable Analytics in Cookie settings. After consent, the site sends page path, hostname, referring origin, campaign parameters, approved interaction events, and a random session identifier. The identifier expires after 30 minutes. Cloudflare Analytics Engine retains those analytics records for up to three months. DLBR does not use advertising cookies, third-party tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or verification data to build advertising profiles.

You can reject analytics or withdraw consent at any time through **Cookie settings** in the site footer. Withdrawing consent does not affect access to the website or DLBR EID. The site then stops sending optional analytics.

## Retention

DLBR keeps personal data only for as long as the relevant purpose requires, then deletes or anonymises it unless law requires further storage. Current service retention criteria include:

- Verification claims and details remain in a temporary session for up to 30 seconds for result delivery, then expire or are deleted.
- Closed support-ticket message content is deleted after 90 days under the service retention process. Ticket metadata may remain where needed to manage support and security.
- Consented website analytics remain in Cloudflare Analytics Engine for up to three months.
- Browser diagnostic reports are retained as a rolling set of up to 500 reports; newer reports replace older reports.
- Account, contact, invoice, and payment records remain while the business relationship is active and afterward as needed for accounting obligations, disputes, and legal claims.

Where DLBR processes verification data for a customer, the DPA and that customer's retention instructions govern the applicable retention period. Backup copies may remain until the normal backup cycle replaces them, with access restricted and restored data subject to deletion instructions.

## Your rights and requests

Depending on the law that applies and the role DLBR has for the data, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability; object to processing based on legitimate interests; and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to a supervisory authority. If DLBR processes the data for a customer, contact that customer first; DLBR will help the customer respond.

Send requests about data DLBR controls to [privacy@dlbr.app](mailto:privacy@dlbr.app). DLBR may ask for information needed to verify your identity, but will not request more than reasonably necessary. Where the GDPR applies, DLBR responds without undue delay and within one month; for complex or numerous requests, it may extend the period by up to two further months and will tell you within the first month. Under Serbia's Personal Data Protection Law, DLBR responds within 30 days and may extend by a further 60 days when necessary, with notice and reasons within the initial 30 days.

You may complain to the Serbian Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection ([Poverenik](https://poverenik.rs/)) or, where the GDPR applies, to the supervisory authority in your place of habitual residence, place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement.

## Automated decisions and security

DLBR returns verification status and related signals to the customer. The customer decides how to use those results; DLBR does not decide whether an individual qualifies for the customer's product or service.

DLBR uses access controls, encryption in transit, tenant separation, and operational audit controls to protect personal data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach affects your rights, DLBR or the relevant customer will notify you when the law requires it.
